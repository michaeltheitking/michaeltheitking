# Hi, I’m Michael King 👋

I’m a technology leader and hands-on engineer based in Austin, Texas. I focus on **digital workplace engineering**, **enterprise automation**, and making complex technology easier for people to use and teams to operate.

During the day, I lead teams responsible for the platforms and services behind a modern enterprise workplace. Outside of work, I experiment with developer tooling, AI-assisted workflows, infrastructure, home automation, and anything else that can be made more reliable with a script.

## What I work on

- 🖥️ Enterprise endpoint engineering for Windows, macOS, and mobile devices
- ☁️ Microsoft 365, Intune, Entra ID, Exchange, Teams, SharePoint, and Windows 365
- ⚙️ Automation with PowerShell, APIs, Microsoft Graph, and Power Platform
- 🤖 AI agents, coding assistants, MCP integrations, and automated workflows
- 🛡️ Device security, compliance, identity, email security, and zero-trust architecture
- 📊 Service reliability, engineering metrics, platform governance, and operational maturity
- 🏠 Home networking, UniFi, smart-home systems, self-hosting, and practical automation
- 🍎 macOS development environments, shell tooling, remote development, and dotfiles

## How I approach technology

I tend to optimize for:

1. **Automation over repetition**
2. **Reliable systems over clever demos**
3. **Simple interfaces over unnecessary complexity**
4. **Measurable outcomes over activity**
5. **Documentation that survives the original author**
6. **AI that can safely take action—not merely generate text**

I enjoy working at the intersection of leadership and engineering: setting strategy, building capable teams, and remaining close enough to the technology to understand how it actually behaves.
